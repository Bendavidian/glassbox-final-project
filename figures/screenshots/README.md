# Screenshots for the architecture report

**Tracked, so they survive a clean clone.** GB-59 established that a figure nobody can
regenerate is a figure that dies with the machine that made it; these cannot be
regenerated by code, so they are committed instead. Earlier captures lived in
`checkpoints/screenshots/`, which is gitignored — they are not in the repository and a
clone has never had them.

Captured by hand at **1920 × 1080**, from a real running dashboard. Not mockups, and not
composites.

---

## The four captures

### 1. `01-overview.png` — the dashboard on open

**State:** `streamlit run glassbox/dashboard/app.py`, deployed configuration untouched,
default view, no symbol selected.

**Must be legible in frame:** the run banner with the **data snapshot date** and the
**deployed band with its selection context** — validation Sharpe **+0.483**, **8 trades**,
grid maximum of 15 candidates. That band caption is required wherever the band appears
(ruled 20 Aug 2026), and a screenshot is one of the places it appears.

### 2. `02-forecast-path.png` — one symbol's forecast

**State:** a symbol selected, the forecast path drawn over the trailing window.

**Must be legible in frame:** the horizon boundary between observed and forecast, the
per-fold band, and the axis in **days** rather than bar indices.

### 3. `03-decision-log-attribution.png` — a decision and its channel attribution

**State:** the decision log open on a record that fired, its channel attribution panel
expanded.

**Must be legible in frame:** the five channel bars, the **residual against 1e-5**, and
the **cancellation flag** if that record trips it. The point of the figure is that the
number on the left equals the sum of the bars on the right; a capture where the residual
is cropped out proves nothing.

### 4. `04-spectral-panel.png` — GB-53, **DEFERRED until after GATE 2's sessions**

**Not captured before the live sessions of 24–26 Aug 2026.** The panel renders only when
`model.active` is `fits`, and the deployed arm is `dlinear`. Switching the active model
the evening before the critical session is a forgetting risk on the wrong evening, and it
would move `config_hash` and trip the drift guard. With the loop stopped after the
sessions there is no running process to drift against and the switch is free.

**The caption is required and is not optional** (ruled 23 Aug 2026):

> FITS is **not** the deployed arm. This panel is shown under `model.active: fits`, set
> for this capture only. The study found FITS beats **neither** reference: direction
> **0.5055** against an always-long bar of **0.5564**, and **+0.31 points** against its own
> white-noise twin.

A screenshot showing a capability the deployed system does not run, without saying so, is
exactly the misreading the panel exists to prevent — committed by the report rather than
by the dashboard.
